    Questions

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Find answers to common questions about our booking process.

    How do I make a reservation?

    Reserving a room at StayEase is effortless. Navigate to our Rooms section, select your preferred suite, specify your check-in dates and guest count, and finalize your booking through our intuitive 4-step reservation form.

    How can I check room availability?

    You can check live availability right from our top hero search bar or by choosing your check-in and check-out dates within the room selection panel. Our system validates room inventory in real-time.

    What payment methods do you accept?

    We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, and secure online payment methods through our trusted payment gateway.

    Can I modify or cancel my reservation?

    Yes, you can modify or cancel your reservation up to 48 hours before your check-in date for a full refund.

    What is included in the room rate?

    Room rates include accommodation, basic amenities, complimentary WiFi, and access to our facilities. Some rooms include breakfast and other premium services.

    Do you offer group bookings?

    Yes! We offer special rates for group bookings. Please contact our concierge team for customized packages and group reservation details.

    What is your cancellation policy?

    Cancellations made 48 hours before check-in are eligible for a full refund. Cancellations within 48 hours are subject to a one-night charge.

    How do I contact customer support?

    Our concierge team is available 24/7 via phone, email, or through our contact form. We're here to help with any inquiries or special requests.
